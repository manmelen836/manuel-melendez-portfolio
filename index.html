About Me

I am currently studying Cybersecurity at Dona Ana Branch Community College. I also hold a bachelor's degree in Criminal Justice from New Mexico State University and have more than 25 years of experience in law enforcement, with over half of that time spent in a supervisory role. My current focus is building stronger cybersecurity and information technology skills that I can combine with the experience I have gained throughout my career. I am especially interested in learning how technology, security, and problem-solving can work together in professional settings. Outside of school and work, I enjoy spending time with my family and staying healthy by going to the gym. My long-term goal is to finish my education in cybersecurity and find an employer where I can use both my current professional experience and the new technical skills I am developing.
